
# Task 8 – Business Performance Analysis using Tree Map

## Objective

Analyze the contribution of different business categories using a Tree Map in Tableau and identify the categories that have the greatest impact on business performance.

## Visualizations Created

1. Sales Tree Map
2. Sales by Category
3. Profit by Region
4. Sales over Time

## Five Business Insights

1. Technology has the highest sales contribution among the three main categories.
2. Furniture has the second-highest sales contribution.
3. Office Supplies has lower sales compared with Technology and Furniture.
4. The Central region shows the highest profit compared with the other regions.
5. Sales increased steadily from 2011 to 2014, showing positive business growth over time.

## Business Recommendations

1. The business should focus more on the Technology category because it contributes the highest sales.
2. The company should continue strengthening high-profit regions such as Central while improving the performance of lower-profit regions.

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides a clear view of business performance using a Tree Map and other visualizations. The analysis shows that Technology is the strongest contributor to sales, while the Central region has the highest profit. Sales also show a steady increase over the years. These insights can help the business focus on high-performing categories and regions and improve overall business performance.
